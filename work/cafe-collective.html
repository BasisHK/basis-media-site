04 · UGC engine

Café collective

A rolling UGC program inside a broader always-on marketing system — six independent cafés, 300+ assets a quarter, none of which look like an ad.

UGC engine

The brief

Six independent cafés, from Sai Ying Pun to Tai Hang, each too small to fund an agency alone and each burning out trying to post daily themselves. Every one of them had the same complaint: the content they made looked like content, and the content that worked looked like a friend filmed it.

So we proposed something none of them had considered — pool it. One program, six brands, shared creator roster, shared production days, split cost.

What we made

A rolling UGC engine. Each quarter we run six half-day shoots with a rotating roster of local creators who actually live in each neighbourhood — the Tai Hang creator covers Tai Hang. Vertical, handheld, natural light, spoken to camera in whatever language that creator's audience speaks.

Behind it sits the boring infrastructure that makes it work: a shared brief template, an asset library each café can pull from any morning, AI-assisted variant generation so one strong hook becomes eight platform-native cuts, and a monthly review where we kill what's flat. 300+ assets a quarter across the collective, and no café ever opens the app wondering what to post.

The result

Average engagement across the collective sits at 9.2% — roughly triple what these accounts were doing on their own content, and above what any of them could buy from a single influencer deal.

Two of the six have opened a second location since the program started. Correlation, not proof — but they both credit the queue.
