04 · Always-on program

Café collective

A multi-brand always-on marketing program — shared UGC engine, AI variant system and agency governance — for six independent cafés that couldn't fund a full retainer alone.

Always-on program

The challenge

Six independent cafés from Sai Ying Pun to Tai Hang faced the same marketing bind: too small for a traditional agency retainer, too busy to run social properly, and tired of content that looked like an ad or never posted at all.

They didn't need another one-off shoot. They needed an always-on system — creative volume, brand consistency and performance review — at a cost they could share.

The strategy

Pool the brief. One agency program, six brands, shared creator roster, shared production calendar, split cost. UGC as the creative language because café audiences punish polish; agency process as the operating system because volume without governance is just noise.

Each café kept its voice. The collective shared infrastructure: briefing templates, asset libraries, AI variant pipelines and a monthly performance kill meeting.

The campaign

Quarterly production sprints: six half-day shoots with neighbourhood creators who actually drink there. Vertical, handheld, natural light, bilingual when needed. One strong capture becomes eight platform-native cuts through AI-assisted variants — then humans pick what ships.

Always-on publishing calendars, light paid boosts on top-performing local posts, and a shared dashboard so owners see what's working without living in Ads Manager. UGC stays the face of the work; the agency runs the marketing machine behind it.

The results

300+ assets a quarter across the collective. Average engagement 9.2% — roughly triple their previous organic baseline, and above what any single influencer buy had delivered.

Two of the six opened a second location after the program launched. Correlation, not proof — but both credited the queue.
