03 · Full-funnel campaign

Sheung Wan Boutique

Hero film, local creator seeding, and paid social — a first drop that sold out in 72 hours at 4.1x ROAS.

Full-funnel campaign

The brief

A Sheung Wan boutique with beautiful product, a Tai Ping Shan Street shopfront, and an Instagram grid that looked like everybody else's. They had one shot at their first proper drop — 400 units, made locally, with cash tied up in the run. If it sat, it hurt.

The brief was uncomfortable and honest: this can't be a mood board campaign. It needs to sell 400 units in a week.

What we made

One hero film, 70 seconds, shot across a single Sheung Wan morning — the street waking up, the roller shutter going, product in real light on real people from the neighbourhood. No studio white cyc. The city was the set, because the city is the reason anyone cares about this brand.

Then we broke it into a funnel. Nine creator-seeded cuts placed with local stylists and Tai Ping Shan regulars who genuinely shop the street — not influencers airlifted in. A paid social layer with three hooks tested against each other in the first 36 hours, then all budget shifted to the winner. Retargeting on watch-through, not clicks, because the film did the persuading.

The result

The drop sold out in 72 hours. Blended ROAS landed at 4.1x, and the winning hook — a nine-second street shot with no product in frame until second six — is now the template for their evergreen creative.

They've since run three more drops on the same system. Two sold out faster.
