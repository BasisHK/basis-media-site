03 · Launch campaign

Sheung Wan Boutique

A full-funnel product launch — creative platform, local UGC seeding and paid media — that sold out 400 units in 72 hours at 4.1x ROAS.

Launch campaign

The challenge

Beautiful product. Tai Ping Shan shopfront. An Instagram grid that looked like every other boutique in the neighbourhood. Their first proper drop — 400 locally made units — had cash tied up in the run. If it sat, it hurt.

The brief was a commercial one, not a brand-film one: sell through in a week, with creative that could also fund the next drop.

The strategy

We ran a classic agency launch architecture with modern creative: hero brand film for desire, UGC seeding for trust, paid social for conversion. Platform idea — the city is the fitting room — kept the work local and specific instead of mood-board generic.

Creative testing was built into media from day one. Three hooks, 36-hour bake-off, then all budget behind the winner. Retargeting on watch-through, because the film did the persuading.

The campaign

One 70-second hero film shot across a Sheung Wan morning — shutter up, street waking, product on neighbourhood people in real light. No white cyc. Cutdowns for feed, stories and paid placements.

Nine creator-seeded UGC cuts with local stylists and Tai Ping Shan regulars who actually shop the street. Paid layer on Meta and TikTok. Organic and seeded posts warmed the audience; paid closed. AI helped generate hook variants; the agency set the kill criteria.

The results

Sold out in 72 hours. Blended ROAS 4.1x. The winning hook — nine seconds of street with no product until second six — became their evergreen template.

Three subsequent drops ran on the same campaign system. Two sold out faster.
