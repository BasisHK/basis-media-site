02 · Brand platform

後生仔講嘢 / Youngblood

A bilingual thought-leadership platform for two Hong Kong founders — show format, campaign creative and a distribution system built to grow authority, not just episodes.

Brand platform

The challenge

Two young founders needed more than a podcast. They needed a brand platform that could earn attention in a category full of careful nothing — HK business audio that sounded like a bank advert. Guests alone wouldn't fix it. Neither would posting harder.

The marketing problem: build credible founder authority in a bilingual city, then turn that attention into relationships and pipeline.

The strategy

We treated the show as the centre of a brand platform campaign. Positioning: talk the way Hong Kong founders actually talk — Cantonese and English mid-sentence, sharp takes, zero corporate gloss. Then design every episode as a content and lead system, not a weekly hobby.

Discovery would live in short-form; trust would live in long-form; business development would live in the guest network. AI handled transcripts and clip mining so the agency could spend time on editorial and growth strategy.

The campaign

End-to-end platform build: format design, Kwun Tong studio setup, two-camera production, edit, mix, artwork and a release calendar. Cold opens drop into the sharpest 20 seconds before intro music. Runtime locked at 38 minutes.

Each record day feeds a weekly campaign pack — nine vertical clips, three quote cards, one long-form YouTube cut, bilingual captions throughout. Distribution mixes organic social, newsletter seeding and targeted guest amplification. The episode is the asset; the system is the campaign.

The results

Top 20 HK Business chart by episode 11. 60k monthly listens across audio and video. Clips outperformed episodes 6:1 as a discovery channel — now the template for every platform we build.

Three guests became clients of the founders' day business. Authority that compounds into revenue.
