粵語定英文？ Stop choosing — subtitle both

Hong Kong's feed is genuinely bilingual and picking one language quietly deletes half your audience. How we script, shoot and cut for 中英 without doubling the budget.

4 min readBasis Media HK

Every few weeks a brief arrives with a false choice already baked in: Cantonese or English. As if Hong Kong watches in one language at a time. As if the person next to you on the tram isn’t switching mid-scroll.

Picking one language isn’t a brand decision. It’s a quiet audience cut. Half the room leaves before the hook lands.

Speak the way the street speaks. Subtitle the way the feed reads. Those are not the same job.

Audio is local. Text is inclusive.

Our default: shoot in the language the talent actually uses with customers — often Cantonese, sometimes English, sometimes a messy mix that is more honest than either alone. Then subtitle for both reading habits.

That doesn’t mean stacking two giant caption blocks that eat the frame. It means designing a bilingual caption system early: primary line for the spoken language, secondary line lighter or staggered, never competing with the face.

Production note

Budget for bilingual captions in the edit day, not as a “nice to have” after colour. AI can draft; a bilingual editor still has to catch tone, slang, and the jokes that die in literal translation.

How we keep cost from doubling

  • One shoot, two reads. Don’t reshoot for language. Rescript captions.
  • Template the type. A fixed bilingual caption style saves hours per batch.
  • Variant only the hook. AI helps generate EN/ZH hook lines from the same master take; humans pick the ones that still sound like the brand.
  • Kill duplicate posts. One bilingual cut beats two monolingual posts fighting each other in the same week.

What “both” is not

Both is not a paragraph of English under a paragraph of Chinese that nobody can read on a phone. Both is not forcing talent to perform a second language badly on camera. Both is respect for how Hong Kong already consumes: ears in one register, eyes in another, brain happily in both.

If your content calendar still forces a language pick before the creative starts, rewrite the calendar. The city already made the choice for you.
