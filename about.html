About the studio · 關於我們

We're Basis Media

A small Hong Kong studio making content that travels for the businesses the city actually shows up for.

We make scroll-stopping work for the noodle shops, boutiques and cafés that make this city worth living in.

We're not an agency. We're a content engine for the businesses Hong Kong actually goes to.

Agencies sell you decks. We sell you the thing at the end of the deck — the video that gets sent to a group chat, the clip that fills your Saturday tables, the drop that sells out before the weekend's over. We start on the street, not in a strategy room, because the best insight about your business is usually already sitting at your counter.

The old model made this expensive and slow. AI changed the maths. We can storyboard twenty concepts in the time it used to take to write one, shoot a month of content in a day, and cut for every platform by the next morning — which means small-business budgets finally buy big-brand reach.

Founder-led

You work with the people who make the work — no account layer, no handoff to a junior you never meet. Small team, direct line, decisions made in a WhatsApp thread instead of a change-request form.

AI-accelerated

AI is our camera crew, not our creative director. It does the volume — variants, drafts, cutdowns, transcripts — so our people can spend their hours on the part machines are bad at: taste, timing, and knowing what Hong Kong will actually stop scrolling for.

Hong Kong-made

We live here. We eat at the places we film. Bilingual by default — Cantonese and English, subtitled both ways — because this city's audience is genuinely split and pretending otherwise loses half of it.

Built as a system

We don't launch campaigns and walk away. We publish, read the retention curves, and re-cut what's working within the week. Content is something you tune, not something you ship once and forget.
